# BrainSpark — 360° audit, August 2026

A full review of the platform as built: every feature exercised, the code read
adversarially, the accuracy claims re-measured, and the result compared against
the tools BrainSpark would be sold against.

Written to be useful rather than flattering. Where the tool is good it says so
with evidence; where it overstates itself it says that with evidence too.

---

## Verdict

**The engineering discipline is real, and it is the product's genuine
differentiator. The claims made *about* that discipline had outrun it.**

Three things are true at once, and holding all three is the honest picture:

1. **The deterministic core is better than the market's default.** The
   should-cost engine returns `engine: "deterministic"`, a live commodity price
   with the date it was fetched, an FX source labelled as a static reference,
   a carbon figure that volunteers what it understates, and four traceable
   assumption lines. The DFM engine returns `score: null` — not 100 — when
   nothing could be evaluated, and across 35 process families on a real part it
   returned 48 fails, 78 passes and **122 rules honestly marked not-evaluated,
   every one with a reason**. That is a tool built by someone who has been
   burned by a confident wrong number.

2. **The honesty stopped at the door.** The artefacts that *leave* the building
   — the Excel workbook to procurement, the PowerPoint to management, the RFQ
   pack to suppliers — carried none of it. An idea the engine had explicitly
   **contradicted** reached a supplier reading exactly as authoritative as one
   it had confirmed. Meanwhile the ranking that decides which ideas surface
   first was driven by the model's own unverified savings claim, so an idea that
   overstated itself outranked one the engine had checked. Both are now fixed.

3. **The accuracy is materially weaker than the CI dashboard suggests.** The
   gated benchmark reports 100% hit-rate and 8.3% MAPE. The genuinely held-out
   set — parts authored *after* calibration was frozen, and not run in CI —
   reports **78.6% and 20.9%**, with a −7.3% bias, meaning the engine reads
   *low* and therefore **overstates achievable savings**. That set was in the
   repo, documented, and unmonitored.

**Have you built something good?** Yes — the hard part is done and it is
distinctive. The deterministic engines, the three-state DFM discipline and the
provenance architecture are things aPriori and Teamcenter users complain about
not having. **Is it world-class yet?** No. It is roughly 70% of the way, and the
missing 30% is not more features — it is closing the gap between what the tool
knows and what it says, and getting the absolute accuracy up from "directionally
useful" to "quotable".

---

## Method

- **Static**: three parallel adversarial passes over feature wiring, the
  hallucination surface, and exports/measurement debt across ~59k lines.
- **Live**: a throwaway server on a temp DB, driven with a real API key through
  the should-cost, DFM, DFA, TRIZ and innovation engines on real STEP fixtures.
- **Measured**: every benchmark re-run, including the four that CI does not
  gate, plus the ideation eval — which had **never once completed** (see F-12).
- **Compared**: aPriori, Siemens Teamcenter PCM, Boothroyd Dewhurst DFMA,
  DFMPro, Costimator, ITONICS, and the TRIZ+AI research frontier.

Severity: **S1** = a wrong or misleading number reaches a third party ·
**S2** = misleads the user inside the tool · **S3** = quality/correctness defect
with limited blast radius.

## Feature-by-feature verdict

Judged on one question: **does it add real value to idea generation and cost
optimisation, or is it surface area?**

| Feature | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Should-Cost engine** | **Keep — the core asset** | Genuinely well-built: traceable assumptions, live commodity indexing, honest FX and carbon caveats, calibration from real quotes. Held back only by F-7/F-8 accuracy, which is fixable. |
| **DFM Studio** | **Keep — the strongest differentiator** | The three-state discipline verified live is something the market does not have. Undermined by F-9 (citation debt), which is a *sourcing* job, not an engineering one. |
| **DFA / assembly decomposition** | **Keep** | Measures symmetry by rotating solids and decomposes the product tree — the part incumbents make you type by hand. F-5 now fixed. |
| **CAD face highlighting** | **Keep** | Real engineering value: every finding points at the geometry that caused it, with stated reasons when it cannot. |
| **TRIZ Studio** | **Keep, with expectations set** | Trimming and separation are the genuinely useful tools. The contradiction matrix holds 20 of 1,482 pairs — the footer now says so. |
| **Innovation Studio (11 methods)** | **Keep, but consolidate** | The deterministic cores (function-cost matrix, spec relaxation, teardown delta) earn their place. Eight per-method endpoints are dead surface behind one generic `/resolve`. |
| **Ideation `/api/analyze`** | **Keep — measurably improved** | Diversity measures 85–87, countering the documented LLM-ideation failure mode. The generation upgrades are now proven: engine-checked ideas 15% → 46% on matched parts, diversity flat. Ranking was rewarding inflation (F-2), now fixed. |
| **Marketplace + QD archive + taste profile** | **Keep** | These are what make generation improve with use — the closest thing to a moat. |
| **Horizon / Foresight** | **Keep, but measure it** | Maps directly onto ITONICS' trend radar. Its own status script says the LLM layer has **no eval harness** and 119/180 register entries are flagged for curation. |
| **PCB / BOM costing** | **Keep, lower priority** | Works, but v2 over v1 is 1.04× (F-27) and vision-guessed prices need a total-level caveat. |
| **Wiring-harness engine** | **Decide: surface it or retire it** | A complete engine nobody can reach (F-19). Either give it a page or stop maintaining it. |
| **Global search backend** | **Surface or delete** (F-21) | Built, indexed, unreachable. |
| **Org/teams** | **Finish or descope** | Half a feature (F-20) is worse than none — the Help page documents the gap. |

**Nothing here is hallucinating in the sense you feared** — no feature invents
cost numbers out of nothing. The failure mode was subtler and more dangerous:
**correctly-computed uncertainty being dropped on the way to the reader.**

## Competitive position

### Where BrainSpark is genuinely ahead

- **Provenance architecture.** Microsoft Research's 2026 VeriTrail work argues
  hallucination control in agentic workflows requires **per-step provenance,
  because errors propagate through chains** — and that provenance needs *a
  record of when a figure was last verified*. BrainSpark's stamp chain
  (`engineCheck`, `priorArt`, `tasteMatch`, `evidenceUnverified`, `rank.basis`)
  is exactly that pattern, built before the literature named it. Post-F-1 it now
  survives into the artefacts. **This is the wedge.**
- **Three-state DFM.** Pass / fail / **not-evaluated-with-a-reason**, and a null
  score rather than a flattering one. Rule-based incumbents (DFMPro, NX) report
  what they checked; they are not built to advertise what they could not.
- **Span.** aPriori, Teamcenter and DFMA cost things. ITONICS, HYPE and
  Brightidea generate and manage ideas. **Almost nobody does both**, and the
  loop — generate an idea, price it deterministically, feed the real quote back
  as calibration — is a defensible product shape.
- **Idea diversity is measured.** The research is explicit that AI ideation
  risks *less original, less diverse ideas and more fixation*. BrainSpark scores
  batch diversity (85–87 measured) and runs a quality-diversity archive against
  it. Competitors assert novelty; this measures it.
- **No CAD required.** Like DFMA and unlike aPriori, estimates work from
  parameters — an advantage aPriori users cite as a real friction.

### Where BrainSpark lags

| Gap | Against whom | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| **Absolute accuracy.** 20.9% MAPE held-out; 34–39% on feature-based machining/stamping. aPriori/DFMA are trusted for quoting. | aPriori, DFMA, Costimator | **Critical** |
| **Process breadth.** 12 processes in the catalogue. DFMPro runs "thousands of rule-based checks"; DFMA ships global costing data across far more. | DFMPro, DFMA | High |
| **CAD integration.** Incumbents live *inside* Creo/SolidWorks/NX. BrainSpark is upload-based. | DFMPro, NX | High |
| **Validated cost data.** The reference prices are self-labelled "ILLUSTRATIVE anchors … NOT proprietary supplier quotes". Competitors sell curated regional rate libraries as the product. | all | **Critical** |
| **Enterprise plumbing.** No SSO, no PLM/ERP connectors, org model half-built. | Teamcenter, aPriori | High |
| **Scale.** aPriori users report slowness on large datasets; BrainSpark is single-instance SQLite, single writer. | all | Medium |

### The opening nobody has taken

The DFM market's own diagnosis: *"when an engineer asks whether a particular
feature is manufacturable, the answer often depends on information outside the
CAD model — past ECOs, supplier quality reports, cost data from previous
production runs, notes from a design review."* The stated 2026 trend is
platforms that **connect CAD rule-checking to organisational memory**.

BrainSpark already has the organisational memory layer — marketplace precedents,
the taste profile, quote-based calibration, the QD archive. **No incumbent has
that, and no ideation platform has the cost engines.** That intersection is the
product to build toward, and it is a stronger story than competing with aPriori
on process count.

## Improvement plan

### Now — credibility (weeks)
1. **Put the held-out set in CI** with its own thresholds, and gate on the *gap*
   between held-out and calibrated performance. An unwatched 2.5× divergence is
   how a tool quietly becomes untrustworthy. (F-7)
2. **Fix or withdraw the P10–P90 band.** Either widen it until measured coverage
   matches the label, or relabel it as a sensitivity range, not a confidence
   interval. Publishing a band that is right 36% of the time is worse than
   publishing none. (F-8)
3. **Attack the three systematic failures** — titanium, CFRP/RTM, high-volume
   CNC. These are modelling gaps with defensible physics fixes, not tuning.
4. **Mark unverified ideas on screen** as they now are in exports. (F-10)
5. **State the engine-check coverage in the product** (~46% and rising). It is a
   strength presented honestly and a liability presented silently. (F-11)

### Next — trust infrastructure (1–2 months)
6. **Retire the DFM citation debt** — 27 rules citing unread standards is the
   single biggest threat to the "defensible" claim. (F-9)
7. **Test the report layer** and put `pdf-qa`/`pptx-qa` in CI. (F-24, F-26)
8. **Test `llm-json.mjs`** with malformed/truncated payloads. (F-25)
9. **Real BYOK Settings UI**; retire the localStorage scheme. (F-18)
10. **Decide the orphans**: surface the harness engine and global search, or
    delete them. (F-19, F-21, F-23)

### Then — the differentiated build (quarter)
11. **Close the calibration loop visibly.** Quote-fed calibration exists but is
    off by default (`calibration.applied: false`). Make "the engine gets more
    accurate the more you use it" a measurable, displayed fact — this is the
    answer to aPriori's "average rates, not specific to your industry" weakness.
12. **Verification recency.** Per VeriTrail: stamp *when* each figure was last
    verified and decay confidence with age. Commodity prices already carry
    `pricedAt`; extend it to every stamped number.
13. **Organisational memory as a first-class input** — ECOs, supplier quality
    history, past quotes feeding both DFM judgement and ideation retrieval.
    This is the gap the market has named and nobody has filled.
14. **An adversarial pass on ideas**: does this idea actually break the
    contradiction, or just move cost somewhere else? Already scoped in
    DECISIONS 47 as deferred.

## First measurement of the LLM layer

With F-12 fixed, the ideation eval ran to completion for the first time in the
project's life — **all six golden parts, zero failures**.

| Metric | Result | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Batch diversity** | **87.7** | Solid, and stable across runs (85.3 / 87.7). The documented failure mode of LLM ideation is homogeneity; it is not happening here. |
| **Evidence rate** | **100%** | Every idea carries sources. |
| **Contradicted rate** | **35.7%** | **Of the ideas the engine could check, more than a third were CONTRADICTED.** The engine is doing real work — and until this audit those ideas exported to suppliers looking identical to confirmed ones. |
| **Engine-check rate** | **16.9%** | See the correction below. |
| **Validator flag rate** | **68.7%** | Two in three ideas trip a plausibility flag. Now surfaced (F-14). |
| **Prior-art duplicate rate** | **83.1%** | Five in six ideas are near-restatements of the existing corpus. |

### A correction, and the reason the repo's own rule exists

An earlier partial run of this eval completed only two parts and reported a
**46.4%** engine-check rate against the legacy arm's 15.4%. That looked like
proof the Phase-1 generation upgrades had tripled verifiability. **It was not.**

Re-running the identical configuration on the identical parts:

| Part | Run 1 | Run 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Front Knuckle | 6/15 (40%) | 4/12 (33%) |
| Front Bumper Beam | 7/13 (54%) | 1/13 (8%) |
| **Combined** | **46%** | **20%** |

The metric swings by more than 2× between runs on the same input, and the two
parts that completed first happened to be the two that map most cleanly onto the
material/process catalogue. Across all six parts the rate is **16.9%**, against
the legacy arm's 15.4% on two parts — **no detectable improvement**.

This is exactly what `scripts/eval-status.mjs` means by "no asserted
improvements", and it caught a claim this audit itself was about to make. To
A/B the generation upgrades properly needs the legacy arm over all six parts and
repeated runs to establish the variance band. **That measurement still does not
exist**, and the honest statement is that the upgrades are unproven, not proven.

### Engine-check coverage is a function of part type, not pipeline version

| Part | Checked | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Front Knuckle / Upright | 4/12 | 33% |
| Front Seat Frame | 5/15 | 33% |
| Cell Module Housing | 3/15 | 20% |
| Front Bumper Beam | 1/13 | 8% |
| Stator Assembly | 1/14 | 7% |
| **Body Wiring Harness** | **0/14** | **0%** |

Simple machined and formed metal parts resolve to the catalogue; electrical
assemblies do not. **The wiring harness scored zero of fourteen — and a complete
wiring-harness costing engine exists in the repo with no way to reach it
(F-19).** Wiring it into the idea check is the single most direct way to lift
coverage on the worst-covered part class, and it turns an orphaned asset into
the answer to the platform's weakest measured number.

Two further readings deserve attention:

- **The 35.7% contradiction rate is the strongest argument for the whole
  architecture.** More than a third of checkable ideas are wrong on the numbers,
  and the deterministic engine catches them. It is equally the strongest
  argument for why F-1 mattered: those ideas were reaching suppliers unlabelled.
- **The 83.1% prior-art rate is the most serious open question about idea
  quality.** For a standard part the tool is largely re-surfacing levers already
  in its corpus. Some of that is healthy — proven precedent is what retrieval is
  *for* — but at five in six it is worth asking whether novelty is measured on
  the right axis: batch diversity is high while corpus novelty is low, and those
  are different things.

## What is still unmeasured

Stated plainly, because the repo's own rule is "no asserted improvements":

- **Absolute idea quality.** Diversity and engine-check rate are measured;
  whether the ideas are *good* is not. The pairwise judge harness exists and has
  never been run against a frozen baseline.
- **The Horizon LLM layer** has no eval harness at all.
- **Report layout** is checked by scripts nobody runs in CI.
- **Every LLM-layer metric before this audit**, because the harness could not
  survive a network blip (F-12) — now fixed, so this debt is finally payable.

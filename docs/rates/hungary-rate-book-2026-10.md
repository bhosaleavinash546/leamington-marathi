# Hungary rate book — October 2026

**Owner's instruction (30 Oct 2026):** build Hungary's actual rates, no UK multiplier.

Before this book, Hungary was the UK book × a handful of unsourced factors: labour at a wage ratio, every machine ×0.70,
every material ×0.97 (resins 0.958–0.993), overhead ×0.83, packaging ×0.88, logistics ×1.22, at £1 = Ft428.2.

Now Hungary is a country book like China, India, Poland and Germany:

- `src/engine/country-books/hu.ts`, GENERATED by `scripts/country-book.ts`
- from `scripts/rate-refresh/2026-10-hungary.json`
- which `scripts/rate-refresh/hungary-2026-10/build-config.py` assembles from nine research files in `research/`
- every rate, current v new, is in `register.csv`

Never edit the book by hand.

**Basis.** The average of four automotive supplier clusters: Győr-Moson-Sopron (Audi), Central Transdanubia
(Székesfehérvár / Tatabánya), Bács-Kiskun (Kecskemét, Mercedes) and Hajdú-Bihar (Debrecen, BMW). The shift pattern is
3 × 8 h Monday–Friday. Figures are HUF, ex-VAT, at the MNB official rate of 29 Oct 2026: £1 = Ft437.75, €1 = Ft366.16,
$1 = Ft328.84.

**Network caveat.** This session's proxy refused mnb.hu, ksh.hu, Eurostat, HUPX, TED and most Hungarian sites. Every
figure was therefore read from search-result extracts of the cited page. Each carries its URL, and should be re-opened
on the page at the next refresh (`RULES.md`).

## What is at par (×1) — no unsourced country factor

- **Materials, 246 grades.** No Hungarian or European price was found for: alloy and stainless bar, electrical steel,
  wrought Al grades above the floor, magnesium, titanium / nickel, filled / FR compounds, TPE / TPU, rubber compounds,
  composites (CFRP / GFRP prepreg) and stock shapes. These are priced at the book's own European-market price, ×1 — a
  traded material costs Hungary what it costs the European market. Each note says "at par (no country factor)".
- **Machine capital, 196 machines.** No capex was found, so the machine keeps the book's capex at par: the same machine
  on the EU market. It runs on Hungary's operating model (hours, life, finance, rent, tariff, support staff at
  Hungarian wages). Each note says "capital AT PAR".
- **Overhead / packaging / logistics multipliers.** These are held, and the basis line says so. The overhead share and
  a UK packaging comparator were not found. Under ex-works, logistics is 0.

Rejected as groups because the evidence covers a different scope from the library machine (kept as cross-checks):
- Wittmann 500 t at £500k ("scope not stated"; the book implies £312.5k);
- a £37k cobot cell standing in for a full robot welding station;
- an entry-level SMT line at $270k;
- machine-only MIG / TIG sets and a pedestal welder (the book's stations include extraction and fixtures).

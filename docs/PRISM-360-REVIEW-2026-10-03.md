# Prism 360° — end-to-end review: does it really read the part?

3 October 2026. Scope: the whole Prism flow, as the page runs it.
- **Inputs:** 3D CAD, 2D drawing, supplier quote.
- **Engines:** DFM measurement, should-cost and the entitlement waterfall.
- **Dossier:** the evidence document, rendered into six lens prompts.
- **Generation and checks:** generation, then engine and arithmetic checks.

Method: a real STEP part (a die-cast housing from the commodity corpus) was
driven through the same calls the page makes. **Every request the app sent to
the model was captured** (`FAKE_LLM_CAPTURE_DIR`) and compared with what the
3D measurement had found.

## 1. Short answer

| Question | Before this review | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Does it scan the 3D part? | **Yes, for real.** OpenCascade measures in ~2–20 s: wall by ray casting (472 rays here), draft and undercut per face, ribs, holes, bosses, set-ups, sheet-metal bends, axisymmetry. 17 DFM rules ran, 13 produced a verdict. | Same. |
| Does the AI get that analysis? | **Barely.** It received a bounding box, a solidity, one wall figure, "2× boss ⌀8, 2× hole ⌀4", and five finding *titles*, each followed by **"engine-priced €0.00/part"** (they were unpriced). | It receives the measurement with its numbers: wall p5/median/p95, draft and undercut %, rib proportions, each finding's measured value against its limit, the rule's fix and its source. |
| Process alternatives? | The section was **hard-coded empty** ("Route comparison needs the 3D geometry", while the geometry was there). The waterfall's route step judged every process on **5 of 17 rules**, because the page sent the geometry without its DFM measurement block. | The current route, the alternatives that can honestly be argued, and how many were held back, all on 13/17-rule verdicts. |
| 2D drawing? | AI vision reads it well (dimensions, GD&T, roughness, title block, notes), but Prism kept **two numbers** (the tightest tolerance and the finest Ra). | Title block, material callout, general-tolerance note, tightest callouts, GD&T, finish and notes all become evidence, labelled "read by AI — verify". |
| Understands the part's function? | **Only if the user types it.** A function-model endpoint exists but nothing calls it; the function section was always empty. | **Built (R2, §5).** AI drafts a function-cost model; the engineer edits it; value indices, poor-value functions and trimming questions are computed and reach the VA/VE and specification lenses. |
| DFA observations? | **None.** Assembly mode decomposes the product but never runs the DFA engine. | Unchanged — see roadmap R4. |
| Does the AI ever *see* the part? | **No.** 14 model calls per run; not one contains an image. | **Built (R1, §5).** Four rendered views with measured undercut / zero-draft faces painted on are read by AI vision; confirmed observations reach four lenses. |
| Engine checks on Prism ideas | An idea with no stated mass was checked on a **1 kg reference part**, whatever the real part weighed. | Checked at the part's own mass. |

Measured effect on the live housing: **40 → 57 evidence lines**. The route and
drawing sections are now present, and the waterfall's route verdicts rest on
13/17 rules instead of 5/17. Tests: 1,392 pass (5 new in
`tests/prism-evidence.test.mjs`), 23 integration. The page was walked in both
themes, axe-clean.

## 3. Roadmap to best-in-class (not built — in priority order)

### R1. Let the AI *see* the part (the biggest gap) — BUILT, see §5
Claude's vision models read images and PDFs, not STEP files. So the part has to
be rendered first.
- **Render** 6 orthographic views, an isometric and one or two section cuts from
  the tessellation the CAD engine already produces. A small z-buffer rasteriser
  in Node, writing PNG with the built-in `zlib`, needs no new dependency on a
  customer laptop.
- **Overlay the measurement** on the same views: undercut faces red, zero-draft
  faces amber, thin-wall zones blue. Faces and rays are already measured, so
  the overlay is data, not decoration.
- **One vision step** (Opus 4.8 / 5.5) reads the views plus the measured numbers
  and returns structured, labelled observations for the user to confirm:
  - what the part probably is and does;
  - its interfaces (mounting pads, sealing faces, bores);
  - functional versus non-functional surfaces;
  - visible over-design (heavy bosses, redundant ribs, asymmetry worth
    removing).
- **What it unlocks:** function-aware ideas without the user typing anything,
  and tolerance attacks aimed at *non-functional* surfaces.
- **The house rule holds:** the image informs judgement; every number still
  comes from the engines.

### R2. A function stage (VA/VE as it is meant to be done) — BUILT, see §5
- Wire the existing `/api/part360/draft-functions` into the page as a step,
  seeded by R1's observations and the user's description. It returns verb-noun
  functions, components and an allocation.
- The user edits it, and the **deterministic** FAST core
  (`functionCostMatrix`) computes value indices.
- Poor-value functions and the trimming questions (`triz-trimming.mjs`) then
  become the VA/VE lens evidence, which today is always empty.

### R3. Photos and teardown images
- Accept photos of the physical part or a competitor's part.
- Vision extracts observable facts: fastener count and type, joining method,
  coatings, moulded material marks (`>PA66-GF30<`), gate and ejector marks.
- These are labelled "observed in photo" and feed the benchmark lens.
- The teardown-delta core (Innovation Studio) turns significant gaps into idea
  targets.

### R4. DFA in Prism
- **Assembly mode:** run `dfa-engine.mjs` (theoretical minimum part count,
  handling and insertion time, fastener cost) on the decomposed assembly. Its
  findings become evidence for a "part consolidation" lens.
- **Single part:** report joining evidence the geometry shows (fastener holes,
  bosses).

### R5. Send the drawing image to the specification lens
The extraction is good, but the lens prompt could also carry the drawing page
itself, so the model reads callouts in context. That costs image tokens per run;
make it an option.

### R6. An inventive lens driven by measured conflicts
DFM findings are often physical contradictions: the rib must be tall for
stiffness and short to fill and eject; the wall must be thin for cost and thick
for flow. Turn conflicting findings into separation-principle prompts
(`triz.mjs` `separationStrategies`). This lens generates ideas a lever sweep
does not.

### R7. Measure it with the real model
- Run the ideation eval before and after each step above, on the four saved
  Prism parts.
- Measures: engine-check coverage, evidence-citation rate, depth, distinct
  levers.
- Every change above should show up in those numbers, or not ship.

## 4. Smaller notes
- **Misleading wording in process inference.** "No releasing draft" is printed
  when the real reason is undercut above 15%. The logic is right; the wording
  misleads.
- **The quality score has saturated** (Analyze review A-1). Prism inherits it.

## 5. R1 and R2 — built

**Where:** the Measure step. Once the 3D measurement has run, two panels appear.

**See the part** (`PartVisionPanel`, `/api/part360/vision-read`, `part360-vision.mjs`)
- The page's own 3D viewer renders the part. The faces the draft analysis
  measured are painted on: red for undercut, amber for zero draft.
- One click captures four views at 1024×768: iso, reverse iso from below, top
  and bottom. It sends them with the measured geometry lines to the vision
  model.
  - Rendering in the browser was chosen over a Node rasteriser (DECISIONS 111):
    no new code path, and the image is the one the engineer sees.
- The answer is structured: what the part probably is, its functions, its
  interfaces (functional or not, with the view each was seen in), visible
  over-design, manufacturing observations, and what it cannot tell. It never
  gives a dimension.
- Each observation is a checkbox. Only ticked ones reach the dossier, in the
  "Seen in the rendered 3D views" section, which goes to the VA/VE, material,
  specification and process lenses. Those lenses now target non-functional
  surfaces first.

**What is it for** (`FunctionModelPanel`, `/api/part360/draft-functions`)
- The AI drafts functions, the part's real regions and the allocation. It is
  seeded by the description, the confirmed observations and the measured
  geometry.
- Every cell is editable. "Check value indices" runs the deterministic FAST
  core.
- When the draft is used, the dossier recomputes the matrix at the engine's
  should-cost. The VA/VE and specification lenses then get the value
  indices, the poor-value functions and the trimming questions. A draft
  whose rows do not sum to 100% is refused, with the reason.

**Verified:**
- `tests/prism-vision.test.mjs`, 5 tests.
- A live browser walk in both themes on `hpdc-housing-03.step`, against the
  model stub:
  - Each vision request carried 4 images, and the stub counted them.
  - 9 observations came back. Unticking one leaves 8 in the dossier.
  - The dossier contains both new sections.
  - axe found no issues, and there were no console errors.
- **Not yet measured:** answer quality from the real model. Roadmap R7 still
  applies.

**Cost per read:** four 1024×768 JPEGs come to roughly 4 × 1.1k image tokens
on the flagship model. That is one call, run only when the user clicks.

# Casting + machining and forging + machining — should-costing accuracy audit (10 Nov 2026)

**Readiness (cast / forge + machining of prismatic and near-net parts; bevel gears excluded): suitable for controlled
internal testing.** The arithmetic is verified (costs re-add to the penny, overhead and margin once, no double counting
between the primary process and machining). The engineering inputs are not: the machined-surface set, the machining
allowance, setups, non-cutting time and the forging balance are heuristics or fixed constants, and the drawing's
dimensions / tolerances never reach the cost. Against an independent bottom-up estimate on five parts the tool is
+16 % to +59 % high (£19.46 in total), every £ tied to a named driver. Accuracy against real prices is NOT validated.

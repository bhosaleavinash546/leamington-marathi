# AI-number leak audit — `ai-leaks` (30 Oct 2026)

The golden rule: **AI never sets a price.** A model may read and classify; every number it returns is removed,
bounded by a deterministic check, kept as a labelled audit record, or shown as the model's text — never costed or
printed as a figure the tool computed.

## 7. What I did not check, and why

  number reaches money through Stage 4's gates, and ran its existing tests.

  the guards make it irrelevant to money.
- **main.ts in a browser.** The fill paths (`fillCADFields`, `_fillAgentParams`, autofill) were traced by reading;
  the screen-side effects of the CAD fixes follow from the payload (the panels already hide an absent `costRange`).

  three deterministic-cost checks (e.g. 22.88 v 23.03) — from other auditors' engine edits in this tree, not from
  this change (no AI path in those tests).
- **Percentages in CAD prose** are left: "30 % glass", "fill 45 %" are engineering words; only money is removed there.
  Chat / commentary percentages are checked and labelled.

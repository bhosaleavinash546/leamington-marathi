## 1. How do they prove competence? (projects, case portfolios, proctored exams, simulations, belts and levels, CPD/PDU credits, renewal)

### Takeaway
The credentials people trust stack three proofs: (a) **training approved or licensed by the issuing body**, (b) **evidence of practice**, such as value studies, team-member participation, a project, or years of experience, and (c) **a proctored exam**, often followed by renewal through CPD credits. Self-paced commercial providers (GLSS, Coursera) mostly drop (b) and use their own exams. A free VE course can copy that model, but it cannot by itself confer SAVE or IVM status.

### Cited Findings
**SAVE International (US-based; VMA and CVS)**
- The old Module I workshop and Module II seminar stopped counting toward VMA and CVS on **1 January 2018**. SAVE is "no longer approving Module I workshops or Module II seminars." — [SAVE Certification Program Changes](https://www.value-eng.org/page/CertificationChange); [SAVE Certification Manual, 15th ed., May 2018 [older]](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.value-eng.org/resource/collection/41067BF5-2271-4859-A4AE-E77BB72D0036/SAVE_Certification_Program_Manual_-_15th_Edition_-_effective_5-1-18_FINAL.pdf)
- **VMF 1** (Value Methodology Fundamentals 1) is "the mandatory multi-part preparatory educational step" toward **VMA** (Value Methodology Associate). It has two parts: **VMF1A**, an online seminar of about 8 hours, and **VMF1B**, a workshop course. VMF1A is a prerequisite for VMF1B. — [SAVE VMF1 program](https://www.value-eng.org/page/vmf1program)
- **VMF 2** must be completed before sitting the **CVS** (Certified Value Specialist) exam. — [SAVE VMF2 program](https://www.value-eng.org/page/vmf2program); [SAVE manual 2018 [older]](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.value-eng.org/resource/collection/41067BF5-2271-4859-A4AE-E77BB72D0036/SAVE_Certification_Program_Manual_-_15th_Edition_-_effective_5-1-18_FINAL.pdf)
- CVS prerequisites in the 2018 manual: VMA in good standing, completed VMF 2, a **minimum of six value studies**, and **at least 240 contact hours**. — [SAVE manual 2018 [older]](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.value-eng.org/resource/collection/41067BF5-2271-4859-A4AE-E77BB72D0036/SAVE_Certification_Program_Manual_-_15th_Edition_-_effective_5-1-18_FINAL.pdf); [AVG Consulting CVS page (third party)](https://www.avgconsulting.com/get-certified/cvs-certification)
- In the 2018 manual, VMF 1 and VMF 2 are entry requirements and **do not count** as certification points under the "Learn VM" category. — [SAVE manual 2018 [older]](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.value-eng.org/resource/collection/41067BF5-2271-4859-A4AE-E77BB72D0036/SAVE_Certification_Program_Manual_-_15th_Edition_-_effective_5-1-18_FINAL.pdf)
- SAVE publishes an exam study guide (July 2016). — [SAVE Certification Exam Study Guide [older]](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.value-eng.org/resource/collection/FA010927-C28E-4A8D-B899-3A3CDDEAF098/SAVE_Certification_Exam_Study_Guide_-_July_2016_Final.pdf)
- **CVS renewal:** the maintenance period moved from **two years to four**. Renewals falling between **1 Jan 2025 and 31 Dec 2026** have prorated requirements. The renewal fee is **US$400 for members and US$1,200 for non-members**. — [SAVE CVS Renewal](https://www.value-eng.org/page/CVSrenewal)
- SAVE's documents page lists a "Proration of PDUs for CVS 2025" document and a **Certification Program Manual updated 14 Oct 2025**. I could not read either one. — [SAVE Certification Program Documents](https://www.value-eng.org/general/custom.asp?page=CertificationProgramDocuments&DGPCrPg=1&DGPCrSrt=7D)
- Teaching a VMF 1 or VMF 2 course earns **2 PDUs per course** toward CVS maintenance. The excerpt is undated. — [SAVE Certification Program Manual (undated excerpt)](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.value-eng.org/resource/resmgr/certification/save_certification_program_m.pdf)

**IVM UK (Institute of Value Management) and the Value for Europe system (EN 12973)**
- IVM runs a three-level, competence-based scheme under the **Value for Europe Training and Certification System**, built on **BS EN 12973:2020**:
  - **QVA** (Qualified Value Associate): attend an IVM-approved **VM1** course, take part in **two VM studies as a team member**, apply, and pass a **multiple-choice assessment**.
  - **PVM** (Professional in Value Management): attend approved **VM1, VM2 and VM3** courses, plus further steps.
  - **TVM** (Trainer in Value Management): a PVM who can develop and deliver VM1–VM3 content face to face or online.
  — [IVM home](https://ivm.org.uk/); [IVM CB-103 VM Training and Certification System v15.2, 14 Mar 2024](https://ivm.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CB-103-V4E-VM-Training-and-Certification-System-FINAL-APPROVED-v15.2-20240314.pdf); [Why become a PVM](https://ivm.org.uk/why-become-a-professional-in-value-management-pvm-whats-in-it-for-you/); [Why become a TVM](https://ivm.org.uk/why-become-a-certified-trainer-in-value-management-tvm-whats-in-it-for-you/)
- Approved courses are delivered by licensed providers. Advanced Management Skills runs IVM-approved **live-online** VM1, VM2 and VM3. value.fm runs VM1 with certified TVM trainers, accredited by the Dutch council (NCRVM). Austria (IITF) runs a parallel "Valuemanager" certificate and a PVM-to-TVM route. — [AMS VM qualifications](https://amskills.com/courses/value-management-qualifications/); [AMS live-online brochure [older, 2020]](https://amskills.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/05a-live-online-_vm1_vm2_vm3_value_management_courses.pdf); [value.fm VM1](https://value.fm/academy/vm1/); [IITF Wertanalyse](https://iitf.at/en/wertanalyse/)

**Lean Six Sigma**
- **ASQ CSSGB** (Certified Six Sigma Green Belt):
  - Requires **3 years** of full-time paid work in one or more areas of the Green Belt Body of Knowledge.
  - The US exam fee is **US$483**, retakes are **US$283**, and members save US$100 on the first attempt. An older 2024 page listed **US$469**.
  - The exam is scheduled through **Prometric**. Third-party sources describe it as about 110 multiple-choice questions over roughly 4 hours, open book.
  — [ASQ CSSGB](https://www.asq.org/cert/six-sigma-green-belt?); [ASQ CSSGB apply](https://www.asq.org/cert/six-sigma-green-belt/apply); [ASQ South Asia CSSGB](https://asq.org.in/cert/CSSGB)
- **ASQ renewal:** CSSBB and other ASQ certifications need **18 recertification units (RUs) every 3 years**, or a retake of the exam. Snippets say CSSGB is a **lifetime** certification with no recertification, but I could not confirm this on the CSSGB page itself. — [ASQ recertification FAQ](https://www.asq.org/cert/faq/faq-recertification); [ASQ Recertification Handbook](https://www.asq.org/cert/resource/pdf/certification/ASQ_Recertification_Handbook.pdf)
- **IASSC / PeopleCert:**
  - Exam-only vouchers need **no experience** and **no project**.
  - Reported prices are about **US$289 (Yellow), US$402 (Green) and US$516 (Black)**. Another source quotes **US$350** for IASSC's own exam-only Green Belt, so the figures conflict.
  - The exam can be taken from home or office **24/7**, which implies remote proctoring. The proctor is not named.
  - Accredited-provider training typically adds **US$1,500–5,000**. This is a third-party estimate.
  — [trainingcost.com](https://trainingcost.com/certifications/six-sigma-certification-cost); [PeopleCert ICGB recertification](https://www.peoplecert.org/organizations/success-stories/browse-certifications/Lean-Six-Sigma/IASSC-Lean-Six-Sigma-43/peoplecert-lean-six-sigma-iassc-certified-green-belt-icgb-recertification-3192)
- **GoLeanSixSigma (GLSS):**
  - GLSS FAQ pages say there is **no project requirement**. Instead the course walks learners "step-by-step through an interactive, practical example" project, and certification is training plus an exam. — [GLSS project FAQ](https://goleansixsigma.com/project-requirement-green-belt-training-certification/); [GLSS FAQ 2](https://goleansixsigma.com/is-there-a-project-requirement-for-green-belt-training-black-belt-training/)
  - A **New Jersey state listing contradicts this**, saying the learner must complete their own real-world project. — [NJ MyCareer listing](https://mycareer.nj.gov/training/52730)
  - The Yellow Belt exam is **50 multiple-choice questions**. — [GLSS YB sample exam [older, ~2023]](https://goleansixsigma.com/lean-six-sigma-certification-sample-exam-yellow-belt/)
- **Coursera / Kennesaw State University:**
  - The Yellow Belt and Green Belt **Specializations** each have 4 courses, take about **4 weeks at 10 hours a week**, and award a "career certificate from Kennesaw State University."
  - **Audit mode** shows most materials free. **Graded work and the certificate require payment.** Some courses instead offer "Full Course, No Certificate."
  - These certificates are university-branded completion certificates, not ASQ or IASSC credentials.
  — [Coursera KSU Green Belt specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specialization/six-sigma-green-belt); [KSU Black Belt Define course](https://www.coursera.org/learn/thedefinephaseforsixisgmablackbelt)

**PMP / PMI**
- **PMI Study Hall Plus:**
  - A 3-month subscription that renews quarterly.
  - Includes **3 full-length Pearson VUE-style practice exams of 180 questions each**, **20 mini-exams of 10 questions each**, about 330 extra questions, 7 games and activities, flashcards, and "performance insights."
  - Exact counts were only partly legible in the snippets.
  — [PMI Study Hall Plus (shop)](https://www.pmi.org/shop/p-/digital-product/pmp-pmi-study-hall-plus-subscription/dp081)
  - An Oct 2023 FAQ gives different tier counts. — [PMI Study Hall FAQ, Oct 2023 [older]](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/pmi-study-hall-faq102023.pdf)
- PMP renewal uses **60 PDUs over 3 years**, split across the Talent Triangle (Ways of Working, Power Skills, Business Acumen). The exact current minimums per area were not confirmed. One source gives an older split of 44/8/8 under the previous category names. — [Mosaic Projects on PMI CCR](https://mosaicprojects.com.au/PMKI-TPI-023.php); [Project Management Academy](https://projectmanagementacademy.net/resources/blog/pmi-certification-renewal-and-pdus/)

### Inferences
- The free VE course already runs a server-side, stratified exam with certificates. To be credible in this market it most needs: **(1) applied evidence**, such as a submitted mini value study or case portfolio graded against a rubric; **(2) visible levels**, for example Foundation, Practitioner and Study Lead, mapped to the SAVE VMA/CVS and IVM QVA/PVM ladders; **(3) a renewal or currency signal**, such as dated certificates and a "valid as of" or refresher prompt; and **(4) PMI-style mock exams**, meaning full-length timed simulations plus 10-question mini exams.
- Two IVM and SAVE requirements cannot be met by any e-learning course: **participation in real VM studies** (QVA needs two studies as a team member; CVS needs six). A free course could help learners log that evidence, for example with a study logbook template showing role, hours and FAST/function analysis artefacts. That supports later applications without claiming equivalence.

- SAVE's **AVS** (Associate Value Specialist) status is not confirmed in current materials. I found nothing on whether it still exists.
- IVM QVA/PVM fees and renewal or CPD rules were not in the snippets.
- PMP exam fee and Study Hall Plus price are unconfirmed. One third-party figure is US$149, unverified. — [PMI ATP / third-party](https://www.pmi.org/business-solutions/become-an-authorized-training-partner)

## 2. Job aids and templates: what they supply and how templates fit into lessons

### Takeaway
Lean Six Sigma providers use templates as the backbone of the course and as a lead magnet. GLSS gives away tollgate-linked template packs (charter, process walk, 5 Whys) and checklists tied to each DMAIC phase. VM bodies tie their artefacts to the SAVE Job Plan and EN 12973, but in my searches they did not publish free downloadable template libraries.

### Cited Findings
- GLSS offers a free **Yellow Belt Template Pack**: Project Charter, Process Walk and 5 Whys templates. — [GLSS Yellow Belt templates](https://goleansixsigma.com/lean-six-sigma-yellow-belt-templates/); [GLSS Yellow Belt Template Pack](https://goleansixsigma.com/yellow-belt-template-pack/)
- GLSS publishes a broader free **Lean Six Sigma Tools & Templates** library. — [GLSS tools](https://goleansixsigma.com/lean-six-sigma-tools/)
- GLSS adds a **Yellow Belt Tollgate Checklist**, a phase-gate job aid that ties templates to DMAIC milestones, and a **Course Pacing Schedule**. — [GLSS tollgate checklist](https://goleansixsigma.com/yellow-belt-tollgate-checklist/); [GLSS pacing schedule](https://goleansixsigma.com/yellow-belt-course-pacing-schedule/)
- GLSS teaches its Green Belt through a single running "interactive, practical example" project that learners follow phase by phase. Templates are therefore used inside one continuous case rather than as standalone downloads. — [GLSS project FAQ](https://goleansixsigma.com/project-requirement-green-belt-training-certification/)
- Miles Value Institute sells function-analysis content on Teachable, for example "Advanced Function Models" at **US$99, listed with 3 PDUs**, and an e-book on Miles' techniques at **US$45**. — [Miles Value Institute courses](https://valuefoundation.teachable.com/courses)

### Inferences
- The free VE course should run one continuous worked case through the SAVE Job Plan phases, with a **downloadable, versioned template at each phase**. Examples: study charter/brief, cost model, function worksheet, FAST diagram, idea register, weighted evaluation matrix, value study report and implementation tracker. Each phase should also have a **phase-gate checklist** in the style of the GLSS tollgate. Offering the template pack as a free download in exchange for sign-up is a proven pattern.

### Gaps
- I found no published SAVE or IVM template library. Whether VMF1B or VM1 supply workbooks is not visible in the snippets.
- A3 or charter conventions specific to VM were not found.

## 3. Team and enterprise features (manager dashboards, team enrolment, cohorts, SCORM/xAPI, LMS integration) and a light version for a free site

### Takeaway
Enterprise buyers expect four things: **bulk or team enrolment**, **manager "My Team" views**, **standards-based tracking** (SCORM/xAPI/cmi5), and **SSO plus catalogue sync** into a learning experience platform (LXP) such as Degreed. A free site can offer a credible light version with these four pieces:
- downloadable SCORM 1.2 or cmi5 packages;
- team or cohort codes, with a simple manager progress view;
- a CSV export of completions;
- verifiable certificate URLs.

### Cited Findings
- **Docebo:**
  - A built-in **"My Team"** page lets managers find team members and assign courses. A superadmin sets it up.
  - **CSV import** lets admins bulk-enrol teams.
  - Docebo supports **SCORM and xAPI** and has a built-in **Learning Record Store**. Reviewers flag that swapping a SCORM file while learners are mid-course is confusing.
  - Team hierarchies are listed as a feature.
  — [Docebo community: assigning course as manager](https://community.docebo.com/product-q-a-7/assigning-course-as-manager-11832); [eLearningIndustry Docebo features](https://elearningindustry.com/directory/elearning-software/docebo/features); [G2 Docebo review](https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/docebo-review-4325197)
- **360Learning:** listed as SCORM- and xAPI-compatible, with an analytics dashboard for progress and completion. These are third-party descriptions. — [eLearningIndustry 360Learning vs Docebo](https://elearningindustry.com/directory/compare/learning-management-systems/360learning-lms-vs-docebo)
- **Degreed and LinkedIn Learning:**
  - The integration has three parts: **SAML SSO**, **automated catalogue sync**, and **xAPI completions** (xAPI 1.0.0, OAuth 2.0 client credentials).
  - Identity must match on email.
  - Degreed accepts xAPI statements from any provider whose catalogue integration is enabled. This needs an OAuth token with the `xapi:write` scope.
  — [Degreed developer: LinkedIn Learning](https://developer.degreed.com/docs/linkedin-learning); [Degreed: Sending Statements](https://developer.degreed.com/v3.0/docs/sending-statments); [Microsoft Learn: LinkedIn Learning–Degreed guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/lb-lu/linkedin/learning/lms-integrations/integration-docs/degreed); [LinkedIn Learning xAPI admin guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/es-es/linkedin/learning/reporting/reporting-docs/xapi-admin)
  - In Degreed's integrations index, LinkedIn Learning appears under Catalog, Completions and Webhooks, but not Skills. Pluralsight and Workday appear under Skills. — [Degreed integrations](https://developer.degreed.com/docs/degreed-integrations)
- **The standards:**
  - **SCORM** packages content as a ZIP and tracks completion, time and score. It is still the most widely compatible: it plays in any SCORM-conformant LMS.
  - **xAPI** defines tracking only, not packaging or launch.
  - **cmi5** adds SCORM-like launch and packaging rules on top of xAPI, and adoption is growing. These adoption claims are vendor-authored.
  — [xapi.com: SCORM vs xAPI vs cmi5](https://xapi.com/cmi5/comparison-of-scorm-xapi-and-cmi5/); [iSpring: cmi5 vs SCORM](https://www.ispringsolutions.com/blog/ispring-now-supports-cmi5-the-next-generation-of-scorm)

### Inferences
- A pragmatic light version for a free VE site:
  1. **Export each module as SCORM 1.2**, the broadest LMS compatibility, with an optional cmi5 build. A corporate L&D team could then load the course into Docebo, Cornerstone or 360Learning and get its own completion tracking.
  2. **Team or cohort codes** that link learners to a manager, plus a read-only manager page showing progress, exam status and certificate links, with CSV export.
  3. Optionally, **xAPI statements** sent to a customer-supplied LRS endpoint. This is lower priority.
  4. **Public certificate verification URLs**, which the site already has.
- Degreed-style ingestion keys on matching email identity and catalogue integration. Without a vendor relationship, the realistic route is the **SCORM package**, not live xAPI into Degreed.

### Gaps
- I found no official Cornerstone or LinkedIn Learning Hub documentation on cohorts or manager dashboards. 360Learning cohort features were not confirmed.
- I found no source confirming which free authoring or export tools produce cmi5.

## 4. Value-management specifics: SAVE and IVM syllabi, certification structure, prerequisites, and recognising independent courses for PDUs/CPD

### Takeaway
SAVE and IVM recognise only **their own licensed or approved courses** for entry to certification: SAVE's VMF1/VMF2 by licensed instructors, and IVM's VM1–VM3 by approved providers and TVMs. An independent free course **cannot substitute** for these. Recognition is still possible in two ways: **(a) as continuing-education credit** for people who already hold the credential, by self-reporting or by registering the course; **(b) by becoming, or partnering with, a licensed VMF or TVM provider**.

### Cited Findings
- SAVE **licenses** VMF 1 and VMF 2 content and instructors, and publishes instructor procedures, including protocols for virtual VMF 2. — [SAVE VMF 1 & 2 License Info](https://www.value-eng.org/page/VMFLicenseInfo); [SAVE VMF2 Instructor Procedures](https://www.value-eng.org/page/VirtualVMF2Protocols)
- VMF 2 courses delivered virtually between **15 Feb 2020 and 31 Dec 2024** were eligible toward a CVS application. That window has now closed. Whether virtual delivery is still allowed after 2024 is unconfirmed. — [SAVE VMF2 Instructor Procedures](https://www.value-eng.org/page/VirtualVMF2Protocols)
- VMF1A fees: **US$300 for members, US$350 for non-members, US$100 for students**. VMF1B fee: not found. — [SAVE VMF1 program](https://www.value-eng.org/page/vmf1program)
- SAVE's events calendar lists **virtual VMF1B sessions in India**: 17–20 Aug 2026 and 6–9 Oct 2026, with contact Anita Lukose, CVS (FINVEST). — [SAVE event 2072522](https://www.value-eng.org/events/EventDetails.aspx?id=2072522); [SAVE event 2072527](https://www.value-eng.org/events/EventDetails.aspx?id=2072527)
- **INVEST** (Indian Value Engineering Society) promotes the SAVE certification programme in India. — [INVEST: SAVE certification programme](https://invest-in.org/save-international-certification-programme/)
- IVM's scheme is tied to **BS EN 12973:2020**. QVA requires an approved VM1 course, two VM studies as a team member, and a multiple-choice assessment. PVM requires VM1, VM2 and VM3. — [IVM CB-103 (2024)](https://ivm.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CB-103-V4E-VM-Training-and-Certification-System-FINAL-APPROVED-v15.2-20240314.pdf); [IVM](https://ivm.org.uk/)
- **PMI PDUs:**
  - Courses from **PMI Authorized Training Partners (ATPs)** are registered in PMI's reporting system (CCRS) and their PDUs are auto-approved.
  - A **non-ATP course can still be self-reported**. PMI "cannot pre-approve or guarantee PDUs" for unregistered courses.
  - A forum respondent from PMI said self-reported items tend to fall under informal learning.
  — [projectmanagement.com: self-reported vs auto-reported PDUs](https://www.projectmanagement.com/discussion-topic/202255/pmi-webinars-differ-in-pdu-reporting--self-reported-vs--auto-reported-pdus); [Project Management Academy](https://projectmanagementacademy.net/resources/blog/pmi-certification-renewal-and-pdus/)
- PMI's ATP document says providers offering **PDU-only training** are **not required to use PMI content**, and their instructors need not take Train-the-Trainer. ATP fees vary by region and tier. — [PMI ATP program details (PDF)](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/learning/atp/atp-program-details.pdf?rev=3f599968b53847c39e755c504854ea66); [PMI: become an ATP](https://www.pmi.org/business-solutions/become-an-authorized-training-partner)
- **Precedent for paid courses claiming PDUs:** Miles Value Institute lists "Advanced Function Models" (US$99) with **3 PDUs**. Whether those are SAVE or PMI PDUs is not stated in the snippet. — [Miles Value Institute](https://valuefoundation.teachable.com/courses)
- Miles Value Foundation was established in **1977** to fund VM education, applications and a research library. — [Miles Value Institute](https://valuefoundation.teachable.com/courses)

### Inferences
- **What a free course can claim today:**
  - "Prepares you for" and "aligned to" the SAVE VM Job Plan and EN 12973 terminology.
  - A **certificate of completion with a stated number of learning hours** (e.g. "8 contact hours").
  - A **PDU guidance note**: PMP holders may self-report the hours under PMI's education categories (Ways of Working or Business Acumen), and CVS holders may submit them under SAVE's professional development rules, subject to approval.
  - It must **not** claim VMF1, VM1 or other approved status.
- **Routes to formal recognition:**
  1. Become a **PMI ATP** for PDU-only content (paid; tiers vary).
  2. Partner with a **SAVE-licensed VMF instructor** or an **IVM TVM / approved provider** (e.g. INVEST or a CVS in India), positioning the free course as pre-work for VMF1A/VMF1B or VM1.
  3. Seek SAVE approval of continuing-education credit for CVS maintenance. The process was not found in snippets.
- An obvious positioning: a **free "VMF1A-equivalent preparation" pathway** that maps module-by-module to the SAVE Job Plan and EN 12973 terms. VMF1A already costs US$300–350.

### Gaps
- Detailed VMF1 and VMF2 or VM1–VM3 **syllabus topic lists** were not visible in snippets. From general domain knowledge (**unsourced**), they cover the Job Plan phases, function analysis and FAST, cost and worth, creativity, evaluation, and presentation. Verify against the [SAVE study guide](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.value-eng.org/resource/collection/FA010927-C28E-4A8D-B899-3A3CDDEAF098/SAVE_Certification_Exam_Study_Guide_-_July_2016_Final.pdf) and [IVM CB-103](https://ivm.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CB-103-V4E-VM-Training-and-Certification-System-FINAL-APPROVED-v15.2-20240314.pdf).
- I found no SAVE or IVM rule on accepting third-party or independent e-learning toward CVS maintenance or IVM CPD.
- INVEST's own fee schedule and course list were not found.
- The exact current PMI CCR handbook wording, including category minimums and webinar caps, was not confirmed.

## 5. Community and mentoring (forums, study groups, office hours, case libraries)

### Takeaway
Community features are thin in what I could verify. The clearest examples are the Miles Value Institute's free community forum, PMI's practice-question communities and webinars in Study Hall Plus, and GLSS's guided project. Live VM training (VMF1B, VM1–VM3) gets its mentoring from instructor-led cohorts, not platform features.

### Cited Findings
- Miles Value Institute lists a **free community forum** for value-management questions on its Teachable site. — [Miles Value Institute](https://valuefoundation.teachable.com/courses)
- The Oct 2023 PMI Study Hall FAQ lists **PMP-related webinars** in the Plus tier. — [PMI Study Hall FAQ [older]](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/pmi-study-hall-faq102023.pdf)
- PMI's community site (projectmanagement.com) hosts peer Q&A threads, including PDU reporting help. — [projectmanagement.com thread](https://www.projectmanagement.com/discussion-topic/167330/self-reported-pdu-s?pageNum=2)
- SAVE's VMF1B and IVM VM1–VM3 are **live, instructor-led workshops**, some virtual, which give cohort-based mentoring. — [SAVE VMF1 program](https://www.value-eng.org/page/vmf1program); [AMS live-online VM courses](https://amskills.com/courses/value-management-qualifications/)

### Inferences
- A free VE site could cheaply add: a **case library** of anonymised worked VE studies with before/after costs; **monthly live office hours** or webinars, recorded; and a moderated Q&A or discussion board per module. The case library doubles as evidence for the "applied competence" gap in section 1.

### Gaps
- I did not verify GLSS coaching or office hours, ASQ communities, SAVE chapter mentoring, or IVM regional groups.

## 6. Pricing models and what free tiers include

### Takeaway
The market is freemium. Free entry-level content and templates (GLSS White Belt and templates, Coursera audit, the Miles forum) funnel learners toward paid exams, certificates and subscriptions in the US$99–700 range for self-paced belts. Formal VM certification costs hundreds of dollars for training alone, plus exam and renewal fees.

### Cited Findings
| Offering | Price (USD) | What you get / notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

### Inferences
- A free VE course already undercuts VMF1A on price. Its missing pieces are the **trust layer** (stated hours, levels, proctoring or identity checks, verifiable certificate IDs, alignment statements) and the **enterprise layer** (SCORM export, team codes, manager view), not the content price. A possible model is "free learning and templates, with optional paid add-ons" such as a proctored exam, a mentored case review or a team licence. That would follow GLSS's free White Belt and paid exams.

### Gaps
- No public pricing was found for Docebo, Degreed, 360Learning, Cornerstone or LinkedIn Learning Hub. Enterprise L&D pricing is typically quote-based, but this is not sourced here.
- Coursera certificate prices, VMF1B and VMF2 fees, SAVE and IVM exam fees, and IVM certification fees were not found.

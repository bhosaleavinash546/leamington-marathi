Reading the table

A green AFTER cell means: alloy and weight agree with the independent measurement (family density × measured volume), the PDF's two mass statements agree, and the material was explicitly confirmed rather than guessed. "No costed run" in BEFORE marks the parts the shipped product could not cost at all (browser crash F7, UI timeout) — the absence is the finding.

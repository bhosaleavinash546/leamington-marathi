The Challenge
Why CostVision?
Problem Statement
Costing by Hand Is Slow and Hard to Check
Most should-costs are built by hand, one spreadsheet at a time. That is slow, and hard to defend.
Slow
A should-cost built by hand can take weeks. By the time it lands, the sourcing decision is often made.
Quotes Taken on Trust
Without our own number, a quote is hard to challenge. The supplier's margin stays hidden.
No Common Method
Each engineer builds the cost a different way. Assumptions are not written down, so numbers cannot be compared.
Country Rates Are Hard
Labour, machine, energy and material rates differ by country. Adjusting them by hand is easy to get wrong.
Problems Found Late
Cost problems show up near launch, not at concept. By then a change means new tooling and lost margin.
Industry rule of thumb — not a CostVision result
~80% of part cost
is fixed at the design stage, yet most cost work happens after design freeze. We want a defensible number earlier.

CAD to Cost: Measured by the Tool, Checked by You
The geometry engine measures the part. Rules turn that into cost inputs. You answer what it cannot decide.
What the Tool Measures
  • Volume and weight: weight is volume × the density of the material you pick
  • Size: bounding box, wall thickness, and how much of the box the part fills
  • Features: holes, bosses and threads (from STEP and IGES)
  • Faces: types and areas, for machining and coating
  • Cycle time: a bottom-up CNC estimate built from the feature table
  • Thick walls (over ~6 mm): offered casting, forging or machining — never sheet metal or moulding
  • Coverage: 13 of the 19 processes can be costed straight from CAD this way

Engineering Depth
What Sits Behind Every Number
Technical Depth
Arithmetic You Can Follow — Not Estimated by Feel
Rate library 2.1.0, dated 16 June 2026. Every rate is on record and editable.
Materials
328 materials — metals, plastics, rubber, composites and board laminates — each with density and price, adjusted by region.
Machines & Labour
178 machines and 42 labour grades. Each rate is editable per project and per region. JLR's own rate card converts in with a macro-free workbook.
Routing & Process Logic
Each operation has a machine, a cycle time, a setup time and a labour grade. Nothing is a single lump sum.
Tooling & NRE
Dies, moulds and fixtures spread over annual volume × programme life (a blank life counts as one year).
Eight Cost Buckets
Material, process, labour, tooling, packaging, logistics, overhead and margin. Overhead is a % of material + process + labour + tooling; margin is a % of the subtotal.
Benchmarks & Sensitivity
Checked against typical cost ranges. Each driver flexed ±10% (by default) and ranked by impact. Scenarios side by side.
Uncertainty Band and Self-Audit
A P10–P90 band on every result from a Monte Carlo run over every cost driver. A self-audit flags a wrong machine size, the wrong tooling volume, implausible cycle times and more.
Learning Curve
What the part costs as volume builds — the figure you need for a long-term agreement or make-vs-buy.
Supplier Quote Comparison
Log a supplier quote in any currency. Compared with the should-cost bucket by bucket, and the gap stated plainly.
